1. Basis of accounting
The financial information has been compiled on the historical cost basis, using accounting policies selected 
by management that are consistent with the company's income tax reporting except as follows: (a) revenue 
on  fixed-price  contracts  over  $50,000  is  recognized  on  the  percentage-of-completion  basis  measured  by  
costs  incurred  to  date  relative  to  estimated  total  costs;  smaller  jobs  and  repair  work  are  recognized  on  
completion; (b) property and equipment are amortized on a declining-balance basis at rates of 10% to 20% 
(shop equipment, cranes, vehicles and mobile equipment) and 45% (computer equipment and software), and 
leasehold improvements straight-line over the term of the lease; assets not yet in use are not amortized; (c) 
income taxes are recorded using the taxes payable method.
2. Nature of operations
The  company  was  incorporated  under  the  Business  Corporations  Act  (Alberta)  on  March  14,  1996  and  
carries on business as a custom structural and miscellaneous steel fabricator and welding contractor from 
leased premises in Nisku, Alberta, serving the oil & gas, agricultural and commercial construction sectors.

6. Related party transactions
The  company  leases  its  premises  (28,000  square  feet  on  3.1  acres)  from  McAllister  Properties  Ltd.,  a  
company wholly owned by the majority shareholder, under a triple-net lease expiring December 31, 2026.

the majority shareholder for bookkeeping and office management. Management salary of $180,000 was paid 
to the majority shareholder. These transactions are in the normal course of operations and are measured at 
the exchange amount agreed to by the related parties.

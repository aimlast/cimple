6. Driver programs
• Entry-level: all new Class 1 drivers complete a 3-week in-house onboarding with a driver trainer, in addition to 
provincial MELT requirements.
• Annual: winter-driving and chain-up certification (October), TDG refresher, reefer pre-trip and temperature-log 
training for reefer drivers.
• Safe-driving bonus: $0.02/km quarterly for zero preventable incidents and clean inspections; paid to 81 of 96 
drivers in Q4 2024.
•  Camera-based coaching: harsh-event and distraction alerts reviewed weekly; 2024 coaching sessions: 412.
